# Decathlon Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end sales analysis project focused on cleaning, organizing, and analyzing retail sales data to identify revenue trends, product performance, and customer demand patterns.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue by product category
- Identify best-selling and slow-selling products
- Measure sales trends over time
- Analyze customer demand by city
- Generate actionable insights for business decision-making

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL
- MySQL
- Data Cleaning
- Data Validation
- Relational Data Modeling

## Data Preparation

The raw sales, product, and customer data was cleaned and organized into a relational structure.

Key activities included:

- Data cleaning and validation
- Structuring sales and product information
- Establishing relationships between datasets
- Checking data accuracy and consistency

## Key Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Category

Hiking & Trekking generated the highest revenue at **₹89,064.25**, followed by Camping & Bivouac at **₹75,039.05**.

### 2. Product Performance

SQL ranking was used to identify the best-selling and slow-selling products based on units sold.

### 3. Sales Trend

Monthly revenue was analyzed to identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 4. Demand by City

Customer cities were analyzed based on revenue and number of sales to identify high-demand markets.

## Key Insights

- Hiking & Trekking was the highest-revenue category.
- CampLight LED Lantern and ThermoFlask 1L recorded the highest unit sales among the analyzed products.
- Delhi generated the highest city-level revenue in the analysis.
- Sales performance varied significantly across months and cities.
